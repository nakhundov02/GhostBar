Unzip the matching build and move GhostBar.app into Applications. These builds are ad-hoc signed and not Apple-notarized; macOS may require approval in System Settings > Privacy & Security. Each friend connects their own account and completes Certificate setup once on their Mac.

Start Proxy, reload the webpage, and press Control + backtick (`) to open or close chat. The shortcut is configurable in the app.

Certificate error -25294 is fixed by explicitly importing into the login keychain. In Keychain Access, select login > Certificates, open mitmproxy, expand Trust, set Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) to Always Trust, close the window, and confirm with macOS.
